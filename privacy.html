Privacy and Cookie Notice

Last updated: June 9, 2026. This page explains how Happy AI Path handles site analytics, contact form data, and privacy choices.

Who operates this site

Happy AI Path is operated by Jim Perry. You can contact us at JimPerry@happyaipath.com.

What we collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit through the contact form or by email, such as your name, email address, organization, and message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and discuss services you request.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded unless you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you click Reject, analytics remains off. You can reopen your privacy choices at any time using the footer link.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, this site treats it as an opt-out request and keeps optional analytics off.

Third-party contact form

The contact form is hosted by JotForm. It is not loaded automatically. It loads only after you click the Load contact form button on the Contact page. If you prefer not to load JotForm, you can email us directly.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, and to object to or opt out of certain processing. To make a request, email JimPerry@happyaipath.com. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers only as needed to operate the website, process contact form submissions, respond to inquiries, and maintain security.
