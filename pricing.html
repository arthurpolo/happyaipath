Engagements & Pricing

Practical AI learning built around your real work

Happy AI Path provides hands-on AI training and one-on-one coaching designed around your actual business challenges, tools, and goals.

This is not training followed by the hope that people will apply it later. The application happens during the engagement.

Custom AI Training & Workshops

Training starts at $5,000

Your team brings real work to the session. Participants complete guided preparation in advance, allowing the training to focus on the challenges, workflows, and opportunities that matter most to them.

During the session, participants solve current problems and build practical prompts and workflows they can continue using afterward.

Most training sessions run two to four hours. Longer sessions and multi-session programs are available.

Final pricing depends on whether the training is virtual or in person, the number and length of sessions, the number of participants, the level of customization, and any travel requirements.

One-on-One AI Coaching

Coaching starts at $5,000

Coaching provides focused support for leaders and professionals who want to complete an AI project, improve an important workflow, or apply AI more effectively to their work. Many clients come to coaching after a training session, with a project they want help finishing.

Each engagement is built around the client’s actual priorities and takes place over several weeks. Clients leave with meaningful progress, stronger judgment, and the confidence to continue independently.

Final pricing depends on the length of the engagement, the complexity of the work, and the level of support needed.

A safe and responsible place to learn

Engagements are designed so people can ask questions, experiment, and build skills without feeling judged.

Training and coaching follow your organization’s privacy, confidentiality, and security requirements. Participants use approved tools and agreed-upon safeguards.
