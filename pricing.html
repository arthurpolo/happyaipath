Custom AI Training & Workshops

Engagements start at $5,000

Every training engagement is hands-on and designed specifically for your team.

Before the session, participants complete guided preparation based on their current responsibilities, workflows, and business challenges. Jim uses that work to customize the training around what the group actually needs to accomplish.

During the session, participants work directly on their own challenges. They test approaches, improve their work, and build prompts and workflows they can continue using after the training.

Most training sessions run between two and four hours. Longer, more in-depth sessions and multi-session programs are available for organizations that need deeper customization, broader support, or continued skill development.

Participants leave with

Completed work
Progress on current business problems made during the session.
Custom tools
Prompts and workflows developed for their roles and responsibilities.
Sound judgment
Greater confidence evaluating AI output and recognizing its limitations.
Repeatable practices
Useful ways of working that continue after the session.
Responsible use
A clear understanding of where AI should and should not be used.

The investment includes participant preparation, content development, and customization, not just delivery time. Final pricing depends on duration, number of participants, level of customization, delivery format, location, travel requirements, and follow-up support.

One-on-One AI Coaching

Engagements start at $5,000

Training creates momentum. Coaching turns that momentum into completed work and lasting capability.

Clients use coaching to develop AI-enabled workflows, custom assistants, reporting processes, and other focused projects. Leaders also use coaching to improve decision-making, communication, planning, and the way they guide their teams through change.

Coaching provides focused, one-on-one support over several weeks. We work with the client’s actual priorities and challenges, moving from an initial idea or opportunity to a practical solution they can confidently use, maintain, and continue improving.

Clients leave with

Meaningful progress
A useful solution, workflow, or body of work that has moved beyond the idea stage.
Greater independence
The ability to evaluate, adjust, and improve what they have created.
Stronger judgment
A better understanding of when to use AI, how to assess its output, and when human expertise matters most.
A clear next step
A practical path for continuing the work after the engagement.

A comfortable place to learn

No one gets put on the spot. People can ask basic questions, try approaches that do not work, and learn at their own level. Content is matched to your team’s experience, from first-time users to people already building AI-enabled workflows.

Happy AI Path provides a psychologically safe environment where people can build skills without feeling judged for what they do not yet know.

Your information stays protected

Engagements follow your organization’s privacy, confidentiality, and security requirements. Participants use approved tools and agreed-upon safeguards. Mutual nondisclosure agreements are available when appropriate.

Sensitive, regulated, or personally identifiable information should not be entered into an AI system unless your organization has explicitly approved that use.
